# bench-top / reveal — round 2

Applied as decisions, not siblings: no bottom-up hints (the bench is just slots and a result). A ? square, tapped twice, names something the player can make right now from what they own, for 1 credit. Discovery gets a full-screen moment again, dismissed by tapping anywhere.

Axis: how the discovery moment plays out. One page, switcher at the top, "replay" re-runs it.
- burst: the icon lands from small to large with an amber glow and 14 sparks flying out, then pair, name, blurb and credit rise in one after another.
- card: a playing card flips from a "?" back to the icon face, then the text rises beneath it. Tactile, restrained.
- curtain: near-black, a thin line of light sweeps across, the name types in letter by letter, then the rest fades in. Cinematic and the slowest of the three.
All three share the same text block and timing scale (100 to 250ms steps). Only the opening differs.
