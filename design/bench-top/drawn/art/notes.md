# art — round 5 (actual illustration, not pictograms in four weights)

Round 4 was rejected, correctly: diagram, poster, sketch and glyph were one family wearing
four coats. All monoline geometry. None of it was art.

Locked: the drawn decision, the layout, the dark chrome, the type ramp.
Free: everything about how a thing is drawn — colour, depth, light, texture, point of view.

Six subjects this time, still chosen to stress rather than flatter: two pure abstractions
(Energy, Gravity), the one that must stay iconic (Star), the soft organic one (Nebula), an
everyday object (Water), and the most detailed thing in the world (Earth).

Shown at 76px where the drawing lives, at 34px in a real card, and at 112px where a
discovery lands. Note the icon grew from 28px to 34px in the same 44px well: line work reads
at 28, illustration does not.

The coherence argument is unchanged and is what makes full colour safe. Emoji are chaos
because thirty artists chose thirty palettes. Here the palette is chosen once, so a
full-colour set is exactly as coherent as a monochrome one.

Gotcha worth keeping: a risograph multiplies ink into paper, and multiply against a near-black
ground produces black. The first render of riso was six black smudges. It overprints with
screen instead, so the inks add light and the third colour still appears where they overlap.
Any print idiom ported to a dark screen will hit the same wall.
