# further — round 6 (wider, still in colour)

Single-ink is out, so riso and plate are gone. Luminous beat paper but was not locked, so it
is carried forward here unchanged as the left column and three new directions are put next to
it. A reference column costs one column and stops the round being judged against memory.

Locked: full colour, the drawn decision, the layout, the dark chrome, the 48px box, 34px in a
card.
Free: the light model, or whether there is one at all.

- **glass** — hard speculars, a lit rim, and a facet seam down each shape. A cut thing has two
  faces; a blob has one, which is what separates this from luminous.
- **enamel** — no light model whatsoever. Flat fields of saturated colour, each fenced by the
  same thin gold wire. The wire is the entire system: it is what would make thirty items look
  like one object.
- **airbrush** — a saturated field, one neon rim where the light comes from, a chrome band
  across the middle. Less an icon than a very small poster.

What the render settles before taste gets a say:

- **glass and enamel survive 34px best**, because both have hard edges. Enamel loses nothing
  at all; its gold wire is the same 1.3px whatever the size.
- **luminous is the softest and pays for it small.** At 34px Energy and Star are both a warm
  glow and the difference between them is nearly gone. That is a real problem for a grid whose
  whole job is telling things apart at a glance.
- **airbrush does not shrink.** The chrome bands that make it worth looking at turn to mush,
  and its Star reads as a sunset rather than a star.

Known drawing bug if enamel wins: the interior field on Water reads as a leaf.
