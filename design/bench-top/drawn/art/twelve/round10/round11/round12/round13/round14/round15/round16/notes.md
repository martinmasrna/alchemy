# round16 — same axis, drawn properly

Round 15's axis was right and all four executions were bad. Those are two different problems
and only the second one was mine. Forty-eight drawings in a single pass is how four columns
end up crude: a face pasted onto a droplet, a word in a box, a wedge cut from a disc.

So: six subjects rather than twelve, spanning cosmology, the everyday, a person and a pure
abstraction, each one actually drawn. The full twelve once a direction survives.

- **compound** — the icon visibly contains the two things that made it. Water has its hydrogen
  and oxygen, Earth is an ocean with an atmosphere over it, Fire has the lightning and the wood.
  This one could only exist in this game, and it spoils nothing, because an icon is only ever
  seen after its recipe has been found.
- **action** — caught mid-verb. The star is igniting rather than shining, the drop is landing
  rather than hanging, the coin is spinning. A grid of these is a grid of things happening.
- **habitat** — the thing plus exactly one piece of its world. A star gets other stars, a drop
  gets the surface it lands on, a person gets a ground and a long shadow. One element, never
  a scene.
- **cutout** — the thing is the absence, punched out of a field of its own colour.

What the render settles:

- **cutout is the most legible thing in the whole search and repeats a mistake already made.**
  Every icon becomes the same rounded square, which is exactly why the illuminated roundel was
  killed in round 8. A grid whose job is telling things apart cannot have one outer shape.
- **compound survives 34px better than expected.** The ingredients read as texture at small
  size and as content at large, which is the right way round.
- **action is the only column with energy in the grid**, and the only one where the six do not
  feel like six objects on shelves.
- **habitat is the calmest and the most likely to get boring**, since the one contextual
  element is a ground line in four cases out of six.
