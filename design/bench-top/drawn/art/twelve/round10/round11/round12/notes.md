# round12 — widened on purpose

Gouache and woodcut won, and Martin's note is the one that matters: they are the same thing in
dark mode and light mode. Opaque colour, textured edge, flat form. Picking two from four
narrows by construction, so two rounds running I had been offering sub-classes of paint on a
surface. The fix is to hold the constraints and drop the family.

Carried forward: expected colour, the twelve subjects, beauty worth celebrating, a support
only where it is earned.
Varied: the material logic. None of these four is a way of applying pigment.

- **clay** — a solid object, modelled and lit. One light high and left, a core shadow opposite,
  a contact shadow underneath. No support, so the grid stays dark.
- **mosaic** — an image that only exists because separate pieces sit next to each other. The
  picture is quantised before it is drawn and the grout is part of it. Every edge is a stair.
- **embroidery** — thread pulled through cloth, so the cloth is drawn. Satin stitch for fill,
  chain stitch for outline, weave showing wherever the thread is not.
- **ceramic** — a fired glass skin, and the first glossy surface in the whole search. Glaze
  pools dark where the form turns away, holds a hard specular where it does not, crazes finely.

What the render settles:

- **clay loses its accents.** Everything in an icon takes the same light model, so the Bitcoin
  glyph and the Calculus curve nearly disappear into the body. Dimensional form and internal
  detail are pulling against each other, and at 34px the detail loses.
- **mosaic and embroidery carry detail best**, because quantising and stitching both keep every
  part visibly separate whatever tone it is.
- **ceramic's crackle is noise at 34px.** It is the whole charm at 72px and clutter in the grid.
- **only clay and mosaic leave the grid dark.** Embroidery brings cloth, which is the same
  trade as woodcut and inkwash.
