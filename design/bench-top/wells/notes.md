# wells — round 2 (palette and type)

Locked: layout, spacing scale, type ramp, content, mechanics.
Free: the whole visual language — ground, plate, accent, icon treatment, type colour and weight.

Measurement that opened the round: the screen carries nine colour tokens, but the accent is
spent five ways (credits, the goal, a picked card, an armed square, the discovery glow), and
next to it sit 31 emoji with 31 unrelated palettes. Colour on this screen is not a decision
anyone made.

The answer here: leave the emoji alone and give them a container. Every icon sits in an
identical 44px round well, so the grid's rhythm comes from the well and not from the glyph.
Chrome recedes to near-black, borders drop to 7% white, and amber is spent on credits alone.
Selection is a brighter plate, not a colour.

Cost: the emoji still carry their own hues, so the grid is calmer but not coherent. This is
the conservative option — it improves the screen without deciding what the icons are.
