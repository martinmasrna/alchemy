# paper — round 2 (palette and type)

Locked: layout, spacing scale, type ramp, content, mechanics.
Free: the whole visual language — ground, plate, accent, icon treatment, type colour and weight.

Measurement that opened the round: the screen carries nine colour tokens, but the accent is
spent five ways (credits, the goal, a picked card, an armed square, the discovery glow), and
next to it sit 31 emoji with 31 unrelated palettes. Colour on this screen is not a decision
anyone made.

The answer here: invert the ground. Emoji are drawn to sit on white, so put them on white.
A warm paper field, cards as tiles with a hairline and a whisper of shadow, ink-black type,
one rust accent doing credits, selection and the armed square. The discovery name is set in
the system serif, the only display move on the screen.

Cost: it stops looking like a game about the universe and starts looking like a field
notebook, and a light screen is harsh for a game people play in bed. The biggest swing of
the three — most likely to be loved or rejected on sight, which is why it is here.
