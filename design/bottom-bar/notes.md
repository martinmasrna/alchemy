# bottom-bar — round 1

Axis: where the tap-tap-result loop lives.
Locked: palette, type system, content, mechanics, 3-column grid, chips for named targets, squares for unknowns.
Varied: a fixed bar at the bottom, in thumb reach, holding the two slots and the result. While a card is held the bar grows one row to show the hints. A discovery grows the bar into a sheet with name, blurb and credit, which collapses on the next tap anywhere. A dud turns the result red and shakes the bar.
Cost: the bar hides ~90px of the scrolling page permanently and ~250px during a discovery, so the bottom of the grid needs padding.
