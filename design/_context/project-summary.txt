What it is
A combine-to-discover game played on a phone, one thumb, short sessions. You own a handful of things (Energy, Matter, Star, Water...), you tap two of them, and either a new thing appears with a line of dry, funny, true text, or nothing happens. A world has one visible goal (Earth) and 27 things to find on the way. Hints cost credits, credits come from guessing.

The single screen has to carry
- your credits and progress (⬡ 4 · 12 / 27)
- the goal, with its recipe once bought (🌍 Earth = 🌊 Ocean + 🌬️ Air)
- the two-slot workbench where the pick and the combine happen
- two bottom-up hint buttons that only make sense while a card is held
- the grid of things you own, growing from 4 to 31 cards over a session, newest last
- the undiscovered: 26 unknown squares shrinking to 0, plus named-but-unmade items with a "how? · 5" button
- the discovery moment: icon, name, the pair that made it, the blurb, whether it earned a credit
- quiet feedback: "Nothing happens", "You already made Water this way", hint answers like "Star leads to Supernova", "Black Hole goes with nothing. A dead end."
- rarely: the summit reached, with a stats line; the first-launch name question

Real content to use in mockups
Seeds: ✨ Energy, ⚛️ Matter, ⬛ Space, ⏳ Time.
Mid-game owned set (14): the seeds plus 🔹 Particle, 💡 Light, 🌀 Gravity, 🎈 Hydrogen, 🌫️ Nebula, ⭐ Star, 🎈 Helium, ⚫ Carbon, 🫧 Oxygen, 💧 Water.
Longest names: Solar System, Black Hole, Supernova, Lightyear.
A discovery: ⭐ Star = 🌫️ Nebula + 🌀 Gravity. "The cloud squeezes until the middle gets so hot that atoms start fusing. It ignites. The dark ages are over."
Named targets: 🌊 Ocean (how? · 5), 🌬️ Air = 🌋 Volcano + 🌀 Gravity.

What matters
The tap-tap-result loop is the whole game and it happens two hundred times a session. Every extra tap, every second of blocking, every thing that needs reading is paid two hundred times.
Discovery is the reward. It should feel like something, but not stop the player.
Failure is information, not punishment. It should be visible and light.
The grid is where the thinking happens: scanning what you own for the pair that makes sense.
Undiscovered squares are a promise of more, and a hint entry point. They are not content.

Known clunk in the current build
Every discovery needs a dismiss tap. Toasts vanish or are missed. Selection feel is flat. The ? squares read as noise. Emoji icons vary in shape and weight, so the grid has no visual rhythm.
