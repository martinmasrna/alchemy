# grid-only — round 1

Axis: where the tap-tap-result loop lives.
Locked: palette, type system, content, mechanics, 3-column grid, chips for named targets, circles for unknowns.
Varied: no workbench. The grid is the only surface. A held card lifts (ring, shadow, 4px rise) and a slim action strip appears above the grid with the two hints and a put-down. A discovery slides into the grid as a new card and a banner above the grid shows name, blurb and credit until the next tap. A dud shakes the two cards and prints "Nothing happens." for 1.5s.
Cost: the held card is the only record of what you are holding; on a long grid it may be off-screen, hence the "holding Nebula" text in the strip.
